The engineering question

How do rules
become feel?

A played hand has to be recognized, broken into the cards that actually score, and then paid out in a sequence the player can follow. Each step is simple; the interest is in how they hand off to each other.

I split the project into evaluation, scoring, and presentation, with ScriptableObjects describing the data each one consumes.
