A study in making game rules feel tangible. This Unity recreation connects poker-hand evaluation with a staged scoring sequence and responsive card presentation.

  • Hand evaluationRank and suit dictionaries identify hand types and the cards that contribute to scoring.
  • Scoring & presentationCoroutines score cards in sequence; Unity events drive chip, multiplier, and score feedback.
  • Configurable game dataScriptableObjects describe cards, hands, blinds, and panel behavior.
