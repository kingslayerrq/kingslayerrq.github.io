The engineering question

How do rules
become feel?

A played hand has to be recognized, broken into the cards that actually score, and then paid out in a sequence the player can follow. Each step is simple; the interest is in how they hand off to each other.

I split the project into evaluation, scoring, and presentation, with ScriptableObjects describing the data each one consumes.

01 / Systems

Three parts of one loop.

Hand evaluation

Recognize the hand

Rank and suit dictionaries identify hand types and the cards that contribute to scoring.

Scoring & presentation

Pay it out in order

Coroutines score cards in sequence; Unity events drive chip, multiplier, and score feedback.

Configurable game data

Author it as assets

ScriptableObjects describe cards, hands, blinds, and panel behavior.

</> Inside the code / staged card scoring+

Let the player follow the math.

Each card finishes its scoring coroutine before the next begins. The short interval makes individual contributions visible before chips and multiplier become a final score.
